# Data-quality check: DK closes taken before a moved start (re-statement, no new rule search)

**Status: data-quality re-statement of results that already exist.** Lead-approved 2026-10-28. 0 credits. No new rule,
threshold or split was searched; the same code is re-run with a group of matches left out.

## Why
- **The close.** `upsets.dk_history.collect` prices each match at its "close": the last snapshot at least 5 min before
  the start listed in the close studies' cache.
- **The problem.** The DK in-play pull (reports/upsets_dk_inplay.md) later showed some events' listed start moving after
  that snapshot. For those matches the "close" is older than intended.
- **This check:**
  - per priced match, take the event's start as listed in the **latest** snapshot of either cache, and flag a move of
    more than 1 h;
  - re-state the existing headlines without the flagged matches: #86, #99, #134 and the Portfolio backtest.
- **Coverage.** The in-play cache covers 2023 – May 2026 only, so 2020–22 are "unknown". Results are Sackmann's, through
  May 2026. #86's report also used Kalshi results for Jun–Oct 2026, which aren't in this re-run.

## Reading
- **Few closes are affected.** Only 1–5% of matches per season moved by more than 1 h after the close, and the median
  move is 1.4–1.6 h, not a day.
- **No headline changes sign or verdict without them.** Every early-round DK dog goes from −9.6% to −8.9%. The DK vs
  Pinnacle margin is unchanged (5.12% vs 2.70%).
- **DK-vs-Pinnacle comparisons hold.** Both books come from the same snapshot, so a moved start makes both prices early
  together.
- **Duplicate listings at close time** (2020–24: 27–52% of pairings) are DK placeholders re-listed with a new id.
  Where in-play snapshots exist (2023 on), only 3 closes came from a stale duplicate; they are excluded with the moved
  matches (table at the end). The "latest listed" test is not evidence of staleness, for the reason given there.
- **The moved matches themselves did worse,** e.g. dogs −38.6% on 153 bets (moved + the 3 stale-duplicate closes). The sample is small, and nothing is built on it.

- **"Latest listed" is not evidence of staleness.** A placeholder listed for a later start (a day later, often 00:00) can outlive the real match in any cache, so the test flags most duplicated pairings, with or without the in-play pull's later snapshots.
- **The in-play snapshots decide** (2023 on): where the close's event and the latest-listed one differ, the close's event was quoted in play after its listed start in most cases and the other one almost never (table). A close counts as stale only if its event was never quoted in play while another of the pairing's events was.
- **One bet per pairing:** the priced table has one row per Sackmann match (dk_history.prices pivots on sack_row), so a pairing listed twice still gives one bet.
- **3 closes came from a stale duplicate** (2023–26); they are an excluded group in the headline table, with the moved matches. 2020–22 can't be checked.
