## Verdict

**Luck in both directions on a thin edge, not an edge that vanished.** The run-up to the peak (2025-08-25) was +66.7u against +15.3u expected (luck z +1.4); the 91 bets after it made -36.5u against +15.1u expected (z -1.5). Over all 328 bets, realized (+30.2u) equals expected (+30.4u).

- **The expected line depends on the de-vig.** It values each bet at Pinnacle's *proportional* de-vig, which gives long shots more chance than the power de-vig used in job A and the other studies. At the power de-vig the same bets expect +1.5u (+0.5% per bet), about zero. These 328 bets can't tell the two apart (realized matches proportional; power is z +0.6 away). The steep expected rise after the peak is mostly DK odds > 10: 31 drawdown bets expected +10.7u at proportional and made -31.0u.
- **No sign the edge changed.** At the power de-vig, expected per bet was -0.7% in the run-up and +3.5% in the drawdown. In this backtest Pinnacle's price is its closing price, so expected *is* the CLV against Pinnacle's close; it didn't turn negative.
- **Not the stale lines.** The drawdown's losses are in bets with the books within 5 pts. The 21 drawdown bets with a 5–10 pt gap made +7.6u vs +8.0u expected; gaps over 10 pts are already skipped.
- Job A's rule (first qualifying snapshot) over the same window, books within 10 pts: run-up +21.8u vs +7.1u expected, CLV +0.1%; after the peak +7.4u vs +2.1u, CLV -2.0%. See the last section.
- **What it means:** the backtest is consistent with a 0–1% edge plus long-shot variance. Proposal for the Lead: value the Portfolio's line bets (rule and expected line) at the power de-vig too, the conservative choice, so the expected line doesn't promise ~9%. The forward test on the Portfolio tab stands.

Curve check (bet by bet; the page's weekly curve peaks a little lower): peak +66.7u realized vs +15.3u expected; end +30.2u vs +30.4u.
